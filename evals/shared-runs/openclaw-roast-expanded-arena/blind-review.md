OpenClaw: the personal AI assistant so personal, you must personally survive the installation. It promises your assistant on every device and chat, then arrives with Android, iOS, Kotlin, Swift, Gradle, and several emotional support build systems. The branding has changed from Warelay to Clawdbot to Moltbot to OpenClaw — less a product roadmap, more witness protection. And with token costs, rate limits, and careful configuration, it’s wonderfully autonomous… at generating admin.

It’s not an assistant. It’s a bill.
