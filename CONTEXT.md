# Roastr Creation Journey

Roastr turns public information about a product or repository into a short comedic video through one progressive creation page.

## Language

**Subject**:
The public website or GitHub repository that the creator wants to turn into a video.
_Avoid_: Target, victim

**Research Brief**:
One Markdown document that synthesizes top-level public knowledge about the Subject, emphasizing drama, common themes, and recurring complaints rather than exposing the mass of raw research.
_Avoid_: Research Pack, evidence table, source dump

**Custom Instructions**:
The creator's optional direction for the generated comedy, including desired emphasis, tone, inclusions, and exclusions.
_Avoid_: Prompt, criteria

**Template**:
A reusable generation package containing a video format, speaking persona, joke strategy, creator-supplied image, and fixed Narrator Voice. The initial Templates are Parody and Roast.
_Avoid_: Treatment, theme

**Parody Template**:
A first-person performance from the product owner's persona that exaggerates the product's origin story, motivation, or pitch.
_Avoid_: Roast

**Roast Template**:
An original external comedic persona that points at recognizable facts and signals about the Subject, such as incompleteness, AI-heavy contribution, vibe-coded construction, stale documentation, exposed credentials, or an unoriginal idea.
_Avoid_: Parody

**Template Presenter**:
The original or appropriately licensed character depicted by a Template's image and represented by its Narrator Voice.
_Avoid_: Celebrity impersonation, cloned public figure

**Narrator Voice**:
The fixed synthetic or appropriately licensed voice associated with a Template Presenter.
_Avoid_: Celebrity voice clone, imitation voice

**Joke Guidelines**:
The shared comedic examples and reusable observation patterns in `joke_guidelines.md`, supplied to every script generation alongside the Research Brief.
_Avoid_: Joke Context, factual evidence, verified findings

**Video Project**:
The in-page journey that combines one Subject, its Research Brief, Custom Instructions, a selected Template, and the resulting vertical video.
_Avoid_: Generation Job, workspace

**Generated Video**:
The real vertical video produced for a Video Project from its Research Brief, Custom Instructions, selected Template, and permitted generation inputs.
_Avoid_: Preview, mockup
